Stylized with a lowercase 'p', pandora is a linear, structured, imperative programming language built for the Tower of Babel.
Programs in plaintext are saved with the file extension .pan, and the output of the compiler can be a series of different files. The most obvious use is a binary file, but the compiler can also provide hexadecimal byte-wise and word-wise files, as well as assembly code in plaintext.
